App de dance “Centre art & danse”: app to manage a dance school, where the owner of the school is the admin, can add teachers, parents and students in the app; the app has groups per category and category level, there is a page of creating publications per group and a mini chat in between the people of the school. The app serves as  an intern app to facilitate the communication and the gestion of the school, as the teachers can just write posts and messages in a group which is way easier than writing message to everyone in particular. Also the parents can follow the parcours of their kids. It is a web app, the website can be added as a web app in mobile, and notifications can be turned on to stay updated with the latest news.

Roles dans l’app:

- PROFESSOR (can create posts, (de)assign himself as prof to groups)
- ADMIN (all rights + admin page, can delete posts)
- SUPERVISOR = parent
- STUDENT (lowest privilege, modify courses + followed groups) if +15 can creat account; if no the parent creates its kids in the app
- STUDENT_SUPERVISOR = SUPERVISOR + STUDENT
